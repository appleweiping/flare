## Executed pilot and current evidence

Block1 completed five folds × six arms × four alpha levels × four fixed deadlines, using 8,922 reused development objects and no classifier refit. At alpha=.01, Mondrian envelope has no singleton decisions. At alpha=.05, it emits 67 labels, all TDE, with only five correct. This follows the always-retained TDE constraint: true-class lifetime protection can coexist with very poor precision among emitted rare labels. It is not a reliable-automatic-classification result.

At alpha=.10 and day100, the Mondrian envelope produces 5,103 commitments with 382 errors and consumes a mean 63.56 days across all objects; the pooled envelope produces 8,368 with 820 errors and consumes 25.68 days. Full-alpha terminal Mondrian produces 7,662 with 542 errors, while waiting to day100. These are differently protected operating points, not a Pareto improvement claim. Full matrix, class counts, candidate utility and predicted-label confusion are retained in /workspace/.research/flare-uq/rare-budget-v1/.

Block2 completed all 1,320 allocation cells. The output-only reward learner shows an exploratory scheduling signal at several query budgets, while quality/temporal additions show no consistent gain; pooled regimes include negative counterexamples. The separate public feasibility probe found 0/278 eligible candidates in the original quality-failure remainder, with a known-quality positive control passing and no model scoring. The completed matrix, caveats and scientific decision are in [RESULTS.md](../RESULTS.md). No prospective efficacy, new conditional-risk certificate, or uniform method superiority is inferred.
